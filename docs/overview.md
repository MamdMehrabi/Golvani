# Golvani — Project Overview

## What is Golvani?

Golvani is a social platform that combines short-form public content with private messaging. Users can share their thoughts through short posts, discover and interact with content from others, follow people they are interested in, and communicate privately through direct messages.

The platform is designed to provide a simple and modern social experience while maintaining a strong focus on reliability, privacy, and user control.

## The Problem

Many existing social platforms have become increasingly complex, centralized, and dependent on large technology ecosystems. Users often have limited control over their data and experience, while the platforms themselves can become overloaded with unnecessary features, algorithms, and dependencies.

Golvani aims to explore a simpler approach: a focused social platform where publishing, discovering, and communicating are the core experiences.

## Why Golvani?

Golvani aims to create a social platform that is simple, reliable, and focused on meaningful interaction.

The project is also an opportunity to explore how a modern social platform can be designed and built with a strong engineering foundation, from its backend architecture and real-time communication to scalability, security, and reliability.

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of Golvani is to evolve into a complete social communication platform where users can:

* Share short-form content with a public audience.
* Discover and follow interesting people and communities.
* Interact with content through likes, replies, and reposts.
* Communicate privately through real-time messaging.
* Build and maintain meaningful social connections.

Golvani will start as a focused MVP and evolve gradually based on real-world usage, technical learning, and user feedback.
